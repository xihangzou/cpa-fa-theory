## Authority

1. Current user instructions define scope and manual execution.
2. The designated book's verified original JPGs define content. CS has no supplied JPGs; its accepted PDF-rendered JPGs become authority only after JPG-CS-INV.
3. `workflow/WORKFLOW.md` and `workflow/SKILL.md` are copied unchanged from the user-designated local workflow. `workflow/GITHUB_ISSUES.md` is the recovered Version 3 manual planning policy; `workflow/provenance.json` records origins and hashes.
4. These contracts make book identity, ownership and evidence requirements concrete. An incompatible content/schema decision must be reported to Astra; it never authorizes weakening source fidelity.

PDF text extraction and OCR are planning/draft aids only. They cannot resolve uncertainty over the designated images. Do not use current accounting standards, web content, memories, another book, or previous Anki packages to supplement or correct the pinned edition. Cross-references to other books remain references, not permission to import content.

## Required learner text

Preserve definitions, distinctions, conditions, exceptions, reasons, timing, procedures, formulas, units, debit/credit/amount relationships, intermediate calculations, useful examples, comparison tables and diagram-only meaning. Represent essential figure meaning in text/tables; if that would lose meaning, retain its exact source locator and report any needed media decision. The current public destination does not authorize raw-media publication.

Use chapter `#`, section `##`, topic `###`, subdivision `####`. Each section chunk covers one actual source section or bounded continuation. Do not flatten source sections merely to satisfy a helper limitation. Do not use bold or Cloze syntax in source Markdown. Remove decoration/redundant prose without dropping meaning. Do not rename theory as financial-accounting calculation content.

Workbooks and CS Part B must keep original question IDs, statements/options, verdicts/correct answers and explanations linked. Incorrect statements remain explicitly labelled; they must never become unqualified knowledge. Preserve the source's reasons and useful incorrect-versus-correct distinctions. Do not invent questions, answers, flashcards or learning-point inventories.

CS Part A and Part B have different numbering and source functions. Keep chapter keys `A01`…`A31` and Part B's original `B01`…`B40`, including its original combined 5–7 and 15–16 topic groups. Do not align these IDs with textbook chapter numbers or integrate the two parts.

## Exclusions and uncertainty

The page plan covers all substantive body ranges and selected explanatory front matter. Covers, contents pages, blank/memo leaves and colophons are metadata-only candidates, not mechanically certified exclusions. Inventory must examine every candidate and record reasons. Blank/divider pages within a batch may also be excluded with evidence. Any discovered substantive content outside the plan blocks that source's readiness for an Astra planning revision.

Never assume a printed-page offset. Record physical page IDs separately from printed labels and part/chapter resets. A missing, changed, illegible, misoriented or unassigned required image blocks the affected work.

Use `[UNRESOLVED: page, location, problem]` or `[要画像確認: ...]` while work is unresolved. Resolve all such markers before PASS. Review every included page and all sections/regions against its source before approving a chunk. Structural checks do not prove semantic completeness.

## Assembly

Keep six books separate. Within one book, assemble in the frozen physical/chapter/section order. Remove YAML and only duplicated continuation chapter/section headings; never summarize, paraphrase or deduplicate body text. Fix source errors in scoped chunks and re-review before reassembly. Final handoff consists only of Markdown plus source, task, mapping and QA records.
